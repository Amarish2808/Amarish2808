## 💡 My Methodology
Regardless of the platform, my write-ups strictly follow this professional structure:
1. **Reconnaissance:** Port scanning and service identification.
2. **Enumeration:** Deep-diving into identified services (Web, SMB, DNS, etc.).
3. **Initial Access:** Exploiting vulnerabilities to gain a foothold (User shell).
4. **Privilege Escalation:** Elevating privileges to Root/SYSTEM.
5. **Key Learnings & Mitigations:** How to patch the vulnerabilities exploited.
